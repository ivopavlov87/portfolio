Intro

Hi, I'm Ivo Pavlov, a software engineer based near Seattle, Washington. My curiosity about how things work led me from studying molecular genetics at Washington State University to working in gene synthesis and, eventually, building software. I trained at App Academy before starting my career in software engineering.

I have over six years of professional experience building web applications, improving existing products, and bringing new AI features to life. I enjoy seeing an idea through from the first conversation to something people can use, and working with others to make it useful and reliable. I value clean, well-organized code that's easy to read and maintain. I'm comfortable digging into complex systems and modernizing older software, with an eye toward making everyday work easier for both users and the people building it.

Outside of work, I enjoy tinkering with new technologies and bringing 3D printing projects to life. I unwind by cooking, reading, hiking, or going on walks with my dog. I'm always up for a good board game with friends and family.
